# Mains exam patterns (2025–2026) and mock blueprints

Researched 24 Sep 2026. Official figures come from the SBI and IBPS notification PDFs. Topic mixes come from coaching-site shift analyses, which are unofficial and vary from shift to shift. This file contains no question text.

---

## 1. IBPS Clerk (CRP CSA-XVI) mains

**Status.** Prelims are on 10–11 Oct 2026. Mains is on **27 Dec 2026** according to the IBPS calendar and press reports. The notification itself only says "November/December 2026".

**Revised pattern confirmed.** The official CSA-XVI notification gives **160 questions, 200 marks, 125 minutes**. The CSA-XV (2025) pattern was 155 questions in 120 minutes. The only change is in Quantitative Aptitude, which goes from 35 questions in 30 min to **40 questions in 35 min**.

| Section (notification says "NOT BY SEQUENCE") | Qs | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| General/Financial Awareness | 40 | 50 | 20 min |
| General English | 40 | 40 | 35 min |
| Reasoning Ability (no Computer Aptitude listed) | 40 | 60 | 35 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | **40** (2025: 35) | 50 | **35** (2025: 30) |
| **Total** | **160** | **200** | **125 min** |

- **Negative marking:** 0.25 of the marks for that question. Unanswered questions carry no penalty.
- **Timing:** each section is separately timed, and unused time does not carry over to another section.
- **Selection:** merit is based on mains marks only. There is no interview.
- **Section order:** the table is explicitly "NOT BY SEQUENCE". In 2025 the order seen in the exam was GA → English → Reasoning → Quant, but analyses disagree on this, so treat it as uncertain.
- **Uneven marks per question (2025, reported by analyses, not official):**
  - Reasoning was split into Part A (20 questions × 2 marks) and Part B (20 questions × 1 mark), which totals 60.
  - Quant was split into Part A (15 × 2) and Part B (20 × 1), which totals 50.
  - For the 2026 Quant section (40 questions, 50 marks), a split of 10 × 2 + 30 × 1 would add up. This is my guess and is unconfirmed.
- **Not covered by the app:** GFA, 40 questions (50 marks). Computer Aptitude does not appear in the Clerk mains.

**Topic mix reported in 2025 mains analyses (29 Nov and 2 Dec shifts):**
- **Reasoning (40):**
  - Puzzles and seating arrangements: about 20 questions, in 5-question sets. Types seen: circular, single-row mixed-direction, floor/flat with age, floor with a variable, box-based.
  - Coded blood relation: 3–5
  - Input–output: 3–5
  - Data sufficiency (2 statements): 3–4
  - Conditional / new-pattern coding: 2–5
  - Critical reasoning (inference, assumption, course of action, conclusion, statement-based): 6–8
  - Meaningful word or digit-based one-offs: 1–2
- **Quant (35):**
  - DI: 15–17 questions. Types: line graph with table, table, pie, bar, caselet.
  - Arithmetic: 11–13
  - Quadratic equations: 2–4
  - Number series: 0–5
  - Data sufficiency: 2–4
  - Approximation: 0–5
- **English (40):**
  - RC: 12–18 (2 passages)
  - Cloze-style "inappropriate or replaceable word in a passage": about 7
  - Rearrangement: 4–5
  - Error detection (correct/incorrect sentences): 2–5
  - Word swap: 5
  - Connectors and starters: 2–5
  - Column matching: 3
  - Odd one out: 1–2

## 2. SBI Clerk (Junior Associate) 2026 mains

**Status.**
- Advertisement: CRPD/CR/2026-27/17.
- Prelims: 26–27 Sep 2026 (regular vacancies). Backlog vacancies had their prelims on 16 Sep.
- Mains: "November 2026" per the notification. Coaching sites say 23 Nov 2026.
- The pattern has not changed from 2025.

| Section (order as listed in the notification) | Qs | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| General/Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 35 min |
| General English | 40 | 40 | 35 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 45 min |
| Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 min |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **2 h 40 min** |

- **Negative marking:** 1/4 of the marks for that question.
- **Timing:** each section is separately timed.
- **Qualifying marks:** there is no minimum for individual sections, only an aggregate minimum. Section-wise marks are not maintained.
- **Marks per question in Reasoning:** 50 questions carry 60 marks, and the notification does not say how they are weighted.
- **Not covered by the app:** GFA, 50 questions. The Computer Aptitude share of the Reasoning section is also not covered:
  - Topic lists from the 2025 shift analyses add up to roughly 50 reasoning questions and show no separate CA block. One analysis mentions only "basic" CA questions.
  - My estimate is 0–5 CA questions, and it is uncertain.
  - Historically, before 2023, CA was about 10–15 questions.

**Topic mix reported for the 2025 mains (21 Nov, shift 1):**
- **Reasoning:**
  - Puzzles: 18–22 questions. Types: triangle arrangement with musical instruments, number-based with 18 persons, designation and colour with 8 persons, box with stationery.
  - Machine input–output: 5. It returned in 2025 after being absent from most 2024 shifts.
  - New-pattern coding: 5
  - Coded blood relation: 4
  - Coded syllogism: 3
  - Reverse inequality: 1
  - Data sufficiency: 4–5
  - Critical reasoning (statement-based): about 9
  - Conditional meaningful word: 1
- **Quant:**
  - DI: about 20 questions. Types: table 6, bar 6, caselet 4, line 4.
  - Arithmetic: about 10
  - Approximation: 4
  - Quantity comparison: 4–5
  - Number series: 3 missing-term plus 2 wrong-number
  - Quadratic: 3, plus 2 statement-based
  - Data sufficiency: 2
- **English:**
  - RC: 11–17 (2–3 passages)
  - Para jumble (7 sentences): 5
  - Word swap: 5
  - Double fillers: 3–7
  - Connectors: 4
  - Statement-based error: 3
  - Idioms: 3
  - Word usage: 2

### Uncertainty notes
1. **IBPS Clerk section order** is officially "not by sequence". The app should let the mock order be configured.
2. **IBPS Clerk marks per question:**
   - Reasoning: 40 questions for 60 marks. The 2025 analyses report a 2-mark / 1-mark split, and this is not official.
   - Quant: 40 questions for 50 marks in 2026. The split is unknown.
   - A simpler fallback is uniform marks per question: 1.5 for Reasoning and 1.25 for Quant.
3. **SBI Clerk Computer Aptitude** is 0–5 questions at most, based on 2025 analyses. It is not confirmed.
4. **Shift variation is large:** input–output, number series, approximation and cloze each appeared in some 2025 shifts and not in others. The mock generator should randomise within the ranges above.
5. **The IBPS Clerk mains date** (27 Dec 2026) comes from the IBPS calendar and press. The notification only says Nov/Dec 2026.
